2. What Talorify does

Talorify reads a job posting you are viewing, compares it with a resume you upload, shows a match score and an ATS readiness check, and rewrites your summary, experience and skills to fit the job. Your name, contact details, education, projects and certifications are kept exactly as you wrote them. Talorify can also write cover letters and, on Pro, prepare interview questions, download Word files and fill in application forms. To do this Talorify uses third-party AI services, including Google. What you submit to them is processed under their own terms and privacy policies, as our Privacy Policy explains, and you agree to that when you create an account.

5. AI results: read before you send

Tailoring is done by an AI model and can make mistakes. Talorify is built to reword only what your resume already supports and not to invent experience, but you are responsible for checking every word before you send a resume to an employer, and for making sure it is true. Match and ATS scores are estimates of how well a resume fits a posting and how readable it is to typical screening software; different employers use different systems, and we do not guarantee any score, interview, or job offer.

Plans and limits can change; if a change reduces something you have paid for, we will tell you first.
