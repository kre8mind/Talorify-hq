Getting Started & Setup

4

Getting started with Talorify takes less than a minute:

  1. Visit the Chrome Web Store and click Add to Chrome.
  2. When prompted, confirm by clicking Add extension.
  3. In Chrome's top-right toolbar, click the puzzle piece icon (Extensions).
  4. Find Talorify in the list and click the Pin icon so it is always accessible beside your address bar.

Once pinned, you can click the Talorify icon on any tab to open the side panel.

Talorify accepts base resumes in PDF format. For the highest extraction quality and accuracy, we recommend:

  • Exporting directly from Microsoft Word, Google Docs, Apple Pages, or LaTeX as a text-based PDF.
  • Avoiding scanned images or photographed paper resumes, as optical character recognition (OCR) can miss important formatting or keywords.

To upload: Open the Talorify side panel, click Upload Base Resume, and select your PDF. Free accounts can store up to 3 base resumes; Pro accounts can store up to 15 base resumes.

Talorify uses Chrome's native Side Panel API so it never obstructs your browsing experience:

  1. Navigate to any job posting on LinkedIn, Indeed, Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, or a direct company careers page.
  2. Click the Talorify icon in your browser toolbar.
  3. The side panel opens immediately on the right side of your window, leaving the job description clearly visible on the left.

Talorify automatically reads the job title, company name, requirements, and responsibilities directly from the active tab.

Yes, an account is required so Talorify can securely store your base resumes, manage your daily allowance, and sync your application history across devices.

You can sign in with one click using Continue with Google or create an account using your email address and a secure password. We verify email addresses to keep the service fair and prevent abuse.

Tailoring & Match Score

4

Talorify's tailoring engine analyzes both your base resume and the employer's specific job posting:

  • Professional Summary: Rephrased to lead with the exact technical profile and experience the employer is seeking.
  • Work Experience Bullets: Reordered and rewritten to emphasize relevant achievements, incorporating exact industry terminology and hard skills required by the posting.
  • Skills Section: Aligned with the job's prioritized technical stack and tools, bringing matching competencies to the forefront.

The entire process takes approximately 10 to 15 seconds, and you can review all changes before saving or downloading.

Strictly NO. This is our core promise: Your story stays yours.

Talorify is built with strict anti-hallucination guardrails:

  • It never invents past job titles, companies, certifications, or employment dates.
  • It never claims proficiency in languages, frameworks, or tools you haven't mentioned in your base resume.
  • A modest role (such as "assisted" or "contributed to") is never artificially inflated into "led" or "directed".

Talorify only reshapes what is already true about you so Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) and human recruiters immediately recognize your relevant background.

The Match Score (0–100%) provides a clear, quantitative snapshot of how well your resume matches the job requirements before and after tailoring:

  • Hard Skills & Tools (40%): Matches for specific technologies, platforms, software, and certifications.
  • Core Responsibilities (35%): Alignment between what you have accomplished and what the job entails day-to-day.
  • Industry Terminology & Keywords (25%): Presence of recognized industry keywords and standard domain phrasing.

Talorify also highlights Missing Keywords so you can see which qualifications you may want to mention if you have them.

After reviewing your tailored resume in the side panel, click the Download button:

  • PDF: Exports a beautifully formatted, clean single or two-page PDF built specifically for seamless ATS parsing. Available on both Free and Pro plans.
  • Word (.docx): Exports an editable document if you wish to make custom typography or visual tweaks before submitting. Available on Pro plans.

Downloads are processed locally and saved directly to your browser's default downloads folder.

Autofill & Applications

3

Job application portals (like Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, and BambooHR) repeatedly ask for the same information. In Talorify, you can save your answers once under Saved Answers:

  • LinkedIn profile, GitHub, and Portfolio URLs
  • Salary expectation & target compensation
  • Notice period & earliest available start date
  • Total years of relevant experience
  • Work authorization, visa status, and relocation willingness

When you're on a job application page, click Autofill Application in the side panel. Talorify automatically detects the form inputs and fills them in instantly.

Never. Talorify adheres to two strict rules regarding application forms:

  1. Zero demographic autofill: Talorify will never fill out questions regarding race, ethnicity, gender, sexual orientation, veteran status, or disability disclosures. You remain in full control of these responses.
  2. Never auto-submits: Talorify never clicks the final "Submit Application" or "Send Application" button. You always review the filled form with your own eyes and submit it yourself.

When you submit an application, click Mark as applied in the side panel. Talorify saves the role, company name, date, link, and exact resume version you used.

For your 30 most recent applications, Pro members can also access AI Interview Prep: Talorify analyzes the specific job description alongside your tailored resume to generate likely technical and behavioral interview questions, complete with recommended response strategies in your authentic voice.

Troubleshooting & Extension Help

3

If Talorify does not immediately highlight the job description:

  1. Make sure you are on the actual job description page (not an overview search feed). On LinkedIn, click directly on the job card in the list to load its full details pane.
  2. Refresh the browser tab once and reopen the side panel.
  3. Manual Paste fallback: If the posting is behind an uncommon frame or dynamic modal, you can simply click Paste Job Description in the side panel and paste the text directly.

Chrome's native Side Panel API requires Chrome version 116 or newer:

  • Open Chrome Settings → About Chrome to ensure your browser is up to date.
  • Right-click the Talorify icon in the toolbar, select Manage extension, and toggle it off and back on.
  • Restart Chrome. If you are on an enterprise-managed computer, ensure side panel permissions are not restricted by your organization.

You can delete individual base resumes or application entries directly inside the extension at any time with one click.

To request permanent deletion of your complete account and all records, message our team using the Contact Support button below or email support@talorify.kre8mind.com with the subject "Delete My Account" and we will delete your records within 48 hours.
